# Senior Backend Engineer

**Company:** Ledgerline Payments
**Location:** Remote (US)
**Compensation:** $150,000–$185,000 base + equity

## About the role

Ledgerline Payments is hiring a Senior Backend Engineer to help lead the next phase of our checkout
and settlement platform. You'll own service architecture decisions for the payments API layer,
operate production systems at scale, and mentor engineers as the team grows. This is a
hands-on IC role with real ownership over what ships.

## Key Responsibilities

- Design and own backend services for payment processing, settlement, and checkout APIs
- Lead technical decisions on service decomposition as we migrate off a legacy monolith
- Operate and troubleshoot production workloads running on Kubernetes at scale
- Partner with product and platform teams to define API contracts and failure-handling behavior
- Mentor mid-level engineers through code review and design discussions
- Participate in an on-call rotation for payments-critical services

## Requirements

- 5+ years of professional backend engineering experience
- Strong proficiency in Python or Go
- Experience designing and operating distributed systems in production
- 3+ years operating production workloads on Kubernetes at scale
- Must be authorized to work in the United States; Ledgerline Payments does not sponsor employment
  visas at this time

## Nice to Have

- Prior experience in payments, fintech, or another regulated transaction domain
- Experience mentoring engineers or leading technical initiatives without formal management authority

## What this role is / is NOT

This role is NOT a pure platform/infrastructure role — you'll be writing product-facing backend
code weekly, not only building tooling for other teams to use.

## About the company / team

Ledgerline Payments builds embedded payments infrastructure for mid-market e-commerce platforms. The
backend team owns the ledger, settlement, and checkout-API services that process partner
transaction volume.
